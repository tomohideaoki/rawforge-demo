# Zero-cost automation gates — business focus
Known inputs: note rawtomohide; Instagram @thetomohide Creator, no Facebook Page link; A8.net and Amazon Associates registered; MT5 Windows installed; no XM demo.

## Safe phase 1
GitHub drafts only: one note article, one Instagram carousel, one affiliate comparison research sheet. Manual review before any publishing.
No automatic scraping of logged-in pages, no stored credentials or affiliate IDs, no paid API, no scheduled Actions.

## Phase 2 checks (not authorized)
Instagram: verify current Meta-supported publishing path, professional-account eligibility, app permissions, and whether Facebook Page linkage is required for the chosen login flow. Do not assume a Page is universally mandatory. Confirm with user before connecting.
note: verify supported free publishing integration; until then drafts only.
Affiliate: review A8 and Amazon program rules, approval status, disclosures and compliant links in dashboards. Do not invent links.
FX: MT5 demo account required before forward testing. Do not connect to live account; do not enable automated execution. Strategy must be specified, backtested with spreads/slippage and risk limits; profitability not guaranteed.

## Success metrics
Content: drafts approved, clicks from each channel, inquiries, paid conversions; do not equate impressions with revenue.
Affiliate: qualified clicks, verified conversions, revenue net of costs, disclosures.
FX simulation: sample size, max drawdown, transaction costs, out-of-sample performance and failure cases.

## Approval required
External publishing, account connection or new permissions, affiliate applications, any API/CLI cost, secrets, scheduled workflows, cloud deployment, real-money trading. Default action: stop and request explicit approval.
